============================================================
ABSOLUTE RULE #2 — BUILD A COORDINATE SYSTEM
============================================================

Use millimeters internally.

Establish a vehicle coordinate system:

X = longitudinal
positive X toward FRONT of vehicle

Y = lateral
positive Y toward DRIVER SIDE

Z = vertical
positive Z upward

Vehicle centerline:
Y = 0

Establish a repeatable Z=0 datum using an appropriate structural/floor
reference.

Do NOT simply use the visible carpet surface if different floor sections
have different elevations.

Every important measurement should ultimately be expressed as XYZ
coordinates relative to this coordinate system.
